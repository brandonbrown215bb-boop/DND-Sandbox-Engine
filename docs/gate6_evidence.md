# Gate 6 coverage evidence

The production coverage report is generated by:

```powershell
python src/generate_feature_matrix.py
```

The report includes one row for every class feature and spell record under
`data/core/`. Each row records status, resolver/effects, player path, and test
group IDs. Runtime-curated feature effects are included in the generator's
status mapping so the report reflects the shipped loader contract.

Current generated exposure summary (2026-08-10): 35 mechanics-backed class
features, 158 quarantined class features, 13 mechanics-backed spells, 15
superseded spell records, and 184 quarantined spell records. The registry
contains 14 classes, 9 races, 234 unique item IDs from 347 raw item records,
198 unique spell IDs from 212 raw spell records, 453 unique monster IDs from
460 raw monster records, 4 event tables, and 1 campaign.

Current boundary:

- Mechanics-backed rows are the curated feature and spell cohort exercised by
  the existing logic and integration suites.
- Barbarian Rage is now mechanics-backed through the shared effect resolver:
  bonus-action use applies a timed raging condition, a physical damage bonus,
  and weapon-damage resistance.
- Barbarian Reckless Attack is now mechanics-backed through a first-attack
  choice, Strength melee advantage, and an until-next-turn vulnerability marker
  consumed by the monster attack path.
- Barbarian Unarmored Defense is now mechanics-backed through 10 + DEX + CON
  while armor is removed, with shields retained in the calculation and armor
  restoration returning to the equipped calculation.
- Monk Patient Defense is now mechanics-backed through the same resolver:
  level-2 bonus-action use grants one-turn Dodging, and its per-turn use is
  restored when the monk's turn begins.
- Monk Step of the Wind is now mechanics-backed through the combat feature
  path, with in-shell Dash/Disengage selection and per-turn restoration.
- Monk Martial Arts and Bonus Unarmed Strike now resolve as per-turn
  bonus-action melee attacks through the shared weapon resolver.
- Monk Flurry of Blows now resolves two adjacent unarmed strikes as one
  per-turn bonus-action contract through the same resolver.
- Paladin Divine Smite is now mechanics-backed through the post-hit owner
  prompt, shared combat resolver, and production spell-slot consumption.
- Paladin Lay on Hands is now mechanics-backed through a level-scaled healing
  pool, touch-range ally targeting, partial pool spending, and long-rest reset.
- Paladin Divine Sense is now mechanics-backed through curated Crown monster
  creature types, typed nearby detection, action use, and readable target labels.
- Paladin Defense Fighting Style is now mechanics-backed through a curated
  armored +1 AC contract that does not apply while armor is removed and survives

- Bardic Inspiration is now mechanics-backed through bonus-action ally targeting,

- Warlock Pact Magic is now mechanics-backed through curated pact-level slots,

  timed modifier, teleport destination validation, reaction-only spell gating,
  and Warlock-aware slot display.
- Crown monster attack riders now cross the shared combat boundary for poison,
  restraint, paralysis, armor corrosion, and maximum-hit-point reduction.
- All 14 current class records now create successfully, resolve a deterministic

- Ranger Favored Foe is now mechanics-backed through post-hit target marking,
  concentration ownership, level-scaled once-per-turn bonus damage, and target

- Ranger Hunter's Mark is now mechanics-backed through curated level-1 spell
  access, bonus-action target marking, concentration ownership, and extra 1d6

- Ranger Archery is now mechanics-backed through a curated +2 ranged attack
  contract, with melee weapons explicitly excluded and a production longbow in

  `quarantined`; approved duplicates are marked `superseded`. The generated

- Inventory now rejects unknown IDs, incompatible equipment slots, unowned
  normal-play equips, and backpack-overflow unequips; broader item effects and
  class-specific equipment restrictions remain open. Mechanics-backed
  consumables now resolve through the same validated effect vocabulary and
  consume one owned item.
- The report is generated from production data and source mappings rather than
  a hand-maintained feature count.

The 2026-08-10 logic runner passed 666 assertions after the Paladin Defense,
Ranger Archery, Barbarian Unarmored Defense, Warlock Magical Cunning, Sorcerer
Innate Sorcery, and Artificer Tinker's Magic contracts were added to the
fourteen-class creation/action/persistence matrix; the Mystic psionics
quarantine was also asserted. The focused Crown balance
fixture passed with the four-member level-2 party defeating the Ruins Wight in
five rounds. The presenter-focused validation passed both `combat_victory` and
`combat_defeat`, and the full isolated integration run passed every named
scenario, including `mixed_mechanics_encounter`,
`action_resource_round_trip`, and `character_sheet_contexts`.
Gate 6 remains `in_progress`. The Rogue package and Ranger action-flow fixes
close two confirmed defects, but deterministic coverage for remaining
Barbarian/Monk/Paladin/Ranger mechanics, cohorts C-D, broad spell and item
contracts, Crown monster/condition audits, AI parity, and encounter balance
remain open. The generated coverage report must also reach zero exposed
`missing`, `description-only`, or `test-only` mechanics before this gate can
pass. Human class sampling is optional and nonblocking; automation owns the
breadth gate.
