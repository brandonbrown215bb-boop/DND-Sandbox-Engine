CC0 / Public Domain fantasy sprite stills for Top Down Party Game
All assets below are CC0 (Creative Commons Zero 1.0) / public-domain equivalent.
Attribution is NOT required but recommended as courtesy.

Output layout (under /workspace/sprites_cc0/):
  characters/<class_id>.png
  items/<item_id>.png
  monsters/<monster_id>.png

Stills are 128x128 PNG with transparency, nearest-neighbor upscaled from source pixel art
(or fitted from higher-res CC0 icons).
