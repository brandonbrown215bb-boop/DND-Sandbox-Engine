## Spell access

The production Bard list contains **136 raw spell IDs; 26 are currently available gameplay slices**. These are runtime inventory counts, not an official-list completion fraction. Words of Creation grants its two always-prepared spells through the existing feature boundary. Magical Secrets can draw from other classes' enabled slices.

The [spell delivery register](spell-status.md) is the single per-spell status source. Bard blockers such as Dissonant Whispers' forced movement remain unavailable; Dancing Lights now contributes modeled illumination through the later
CombatPerception path; its complete clauses remain uncertified. No entire spell-catalog completion claim follows from this class pass.

## Verification

Windows Godot 4.7, headless:

- Focused `bard`: **32 passed, zero failed**; `.artifacts/bard-creation/focused-accepted.log` (24 behavior checks plus eight creation/save regressions).
- Full logic: **1,829 passed, zero failed**; `.artifacts/bard-creation/full-accepted/`. Bootstrap, parse and boot prerequisites passed. Earlier Bard fixtures replaced their creation skills and made an inconsistent save; they now preserve those grants. A pre-existing Brutal Strike hit test now seeds its attack so a random natural-one miss cannot fail the hit-specific assertion; its separate miss regression remains.
- `bard_action_flow`: **passed**, with no unexpected engine errors; `.artifacts/bard-creation/accepted-bard_action_flow.log`. It drives the actual sheet, menus, recipient choices, target selection and disk-save/reload boundary, including malformed-clock preflight without live-party mutation.
- `bard_creation_flow`, `barbarian_choice_flow` and `character_creation`: **passed**, with no unexpected engine errors; `.artifacts/bard-creation/accepted-<scenario>.log`. Bard creation covers both alternatives, species proficiency exclusion, duplicate-choice gating, review/back/reset/random behavior, exact inventory and canonical disk reload, player/party preflight and the actual sheet.
- The broader `normal_creation_matrix` failed **17 checks in this 2026-10-05 audit**; `.artifacts/bard-creation/accepted-normal_creation_matrix.log`. It expects superseded class equipment, implicit default choices/spells and Artificer in the picker. This pass does not claim that matrix is green; update those expectations when its broader class flows are touched.

No full integration-suite or manual/rendered acceptance claim. Team role contracts were unavailable; source review and verification were performed solo. Older saves without an Inspiration clock adopt one bounded hour once; their historical elapsed time cannot be reconstructed.

## Remaining scope

Lore, full feats/boons, general instrument Utilize behavior and complete spell
families remain separate gaps. The old creation-matrix failure is evidence from
the dated audit, not a newly reproduced current defect. Work follows the shared
runtime roadmap rather than this audit's former class-by-class sequence.

Use `-LogicGroup bard` and affected live scenarios for changes; broaden once
at a shared-behavior acceptance boundary.
