# Monk 2024: Partial Levels 1–20 Support

> Partial behavior/migration snapshot. Dated evidence and catalog counts
> below apply to their original audit, not automatic acceptance of the
> current tree. [Roadmap](../../roadmap.md) selects work;
> [ADR-0018](../decisions/0018-shared-data-driven-rules-runtime.md) governs
> new rule execution. Existing named helpers are transitional.

Authority: [SRD 5.2.1](https://media.dndbeyond.com/compendium-images/srd/5.2/SRD_CC_v5.2.1.pdf), Monk pages 49-51.

The base progression has playable mechanics through level 20, but the class
and Warrior of the Open Hand remain partial. Open Hand Technique, Slow Fall,
Acrobatic Movement, Quivering Palm and the level-19 Epic Boon choice are
unavailable. Their records are explicitly disabled rather than exposing actions
that spend resources without resolving the rule. Step of the Wind carrying a
willing creature and exhaustion from food/drink are also not modeled.

Open Hand has a separate subclass record. It is automatically selected at level
3 when it is the sole installed option; multiple installed options are chosen
on the Character Sheet. Only the selected subclass grants its features.

## Playable progression

### Levels 1–5 Base Mechanics
- **Level 1 Martial Arts**: No armor training or starting armor/shield. Spear, five daggers and 11 GP.
  Martial Arts operates while unarmored, without a shield, and using only Monk weapons (Simple Melee weapons and Light Martial Melee weapons).
  Dexterity is used for attack and damage rolls. Bonus Unarmed Strike is available as a Bonus Action.
- **Level 1 Unarmored Defense**: AC equals 10 + Dexterity modifier + Wisdom modifier while unarmored and without shield.
- **Level 2 Monk's Focus**: Shared Focus Points equal to Monk level; Short and Long Rests restore the pool. Remaining Focus persists across Character serialization and save/load.
- **Level 2 Flurry of Blows**: Costs 1 Focus and a Bonus Action for two Unarmed Strikes (three strikes at level 10+ with Heightened Focus). Target death ends the sequence; invalid/cancelled targeting spends nothing.
- **Level 2 Patient Defense**: Free Bonus Action Disengage, or 1 Focus for Disengage + Dodge together.
- **Level 2 Step of the Wind**: Free Bonus Action Dash, or 1 Focus for Dash + Disengage together.
- **Level 2 Unarmored Movement**: Speed bonus scales with Monk level:
  - Levels 2–5: +10 ft (+2 tiles)
  - Levels 6–9: +15 ft (+3 tiles)
  - Levels 10–13: +20 ft (+4 tiles)
  - Levels 14–17: +25 ft (+5 tiles)
  - Levels 18–20: +30 ft (+6 tiles)
- **Level 2 Uncanny Metabolism**: On Initiative roll, can recover all Focus Points and regain HP equal to Monk level + Martial Arts die roll (once per Long Rest).
- **Level 3 Deflect Attacks**: Reaction reduces incoming damage by 1d10 + Dexterity modifier + Monk level. Reducing damage to zero permits a 1-Focus redirect at an in-range enemy (Dexterity save vs Monk DC; 2 Martial Arts dice + Dexterity mod damage).
- **Level 4 Ability Score Improvement**: Choose +2 to one ability or +1 to two
  abilities, capped at 20, on the Character Sheet outside combat. Saved feature
  entitlements prevent replay and retain unspent choices. Levels 8, 12 and 16
  use the same controls; Constitution changes update HP for all existing levels.
- **Level 5 Extra Attack & Stunning Strike**: Two weapon/unarmed attacks per Attack action. Once per turn on hit, spend 1 Focus: failed CON save Stuns target until start of Monk's next turn; successful save halves speed and grants next attack Advantage.
- **Martial Arts Die Scaling**:
  - Levels 1–4: 1d6
  - Levels 5–10: 1d8
  - Levels 11–16: 1d10
  - Levels 17–20: 1d12

### Levels 6–20 Progression
- **Level 6 Empowered Strikes**: Unarmed Strikes deal Force damage instead of Bludgeoning.
- **Level 6 Wholeness of Body**: Bonus Action heal equal to 1 roll of Martial Arts die + Wisdom modifier. Uses equal Wisdom modifier (minimum 1) per Long Rest.
- **Level 7 Evasion**: Dexterity saves against damage effects (half on save): 0 damage on successful save, half damage on failed save.
- **Level 8 Ability Score Improvement**: Stat enhancement.
- **Level 10 Heightened Focus**:
  - Flurry of Blows makes three Unarmed Strikes (up from two).
  - Patient Defense with Focus grants Temporary HP equal to two rolls of Martial Arts die.
- **Level 10 Self-Restoration**: Removes one condition (Charmed, Frightened, or Poisoned) at the end of each turn.
- **Level 11 Fleet Step**: Using a Bonus Action other than Step of the Wind also grants the Dash movement of Step of the Wind.
- **Level 12 Ability Score Improvement**: Stat enhancement.
- **Level 13 Deflect Energy**: Deflect Attacks reaction now applies to attacks of ANY damage type (not just Bludgeoning/Piercing/Slashing).
- **Level 14 Disciplined Survivor**: Proficiency in all six saving throws (STR, DEX, CON, INT, WIS, CHA). When failing a saving throw, can spend 1 Focus Point to reroll.
- **Level 15 Perfect Focus**: When rolling Initiative, if remaining Focus is 3 or fewer, restore pool to 4 Focus Points.
- **Level 16 Ability Score Improvement**: Stat enhancement.
- **Level 18 Superior Defense**: Spend 3 Focus Points to gain resistance to all damage types except Force damage for 1 minute (10 turns) or until Incapacitated.
- **Level 20 Body and Mind**: Dexterity and Wisdom ability scores increase by 4, with maximum raised to 25. Focus Points pool reaches 20.

## HUD, Menus, and Integration

- The HUD and Character Sheet show current/maximum Focus Points and Wholeness of Body uses.
- Feature menu lists dynamic Focus costs (`1 Focus`, `3 Focus`, `4 Focus`).
- Wholeness and Fighter Second Wind share the validated healing executor. One
  activation spends one use and one Bonus Action; the final use succeeds and
  repeated clicks cannot heal again. Remaining uses survive SaveManager reload.
- Character reconstruction preserves exact saved ability scores. Body and Mind
  applies on acquisition only; loading no longer grants another +4 DEX/WIS.
- Godot checks verify exact score/cost round-trips. The real viewport fixture
  exercises the Character Sheet and Wholeness combat menu; helper-only tests
  do not establish complete class or subclass support. See the dated checkpoint.
